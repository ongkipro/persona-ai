## Foto Utama

Kita akan buat beberapa angle dari foto ini, caranya kita akan gunakan Chat GPT.
***Cara pakai:***

Upload dua gambar dengan urutan tetap:

1. **Image 1**: foto scene (sumber environment, lighting, pose, outfit)
2. **Image 2**: <mark class="brush-yellow">character sheet</mark> (sumber identitas wajah, rambut, detail outfit)

Kemudian masukan template prompt berikut sesuai dengan kebutuhan kamu.

1. Wide Establishing

```plain text
Using Image 1 as the master scene reference and Image 2 as the character identity reference, recreate the exact same moment from a different camera position.

CAMERA: Wide establishing shot, eye level, camera centered in front of the subject, full body visible, showing most of the room.

KEEP IDENTICAL TO IMAGE 1:
- Environment: same room, layout, furniture, props, wall decor, colors, and object positions. Treat the room as a real 3D space; only the camera moves, with physically correct perspective and parallax.
- Lighting: same light sources, direction, color temperature, intensity, contrast, and mood, with consistent highlights and shadows on the subject.
- Character: exact same person as Image 2. Same face structure, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, skin texture, makeup, hairstyle, hair color, hair length, and hair volume. Facial identity is the highest priority.
- Outfit: same clothing, accessories, fabric textures, patterns, and fit.
- Pose: same seated posture, hand placement, and expression, as if captured at the same instant by a second camera.
- Microphone/equipment: same physical position relative to the subject.

STYLE: Photorealistic, cinema camera look, natural skin texture, realistic depth of field, color grading matching Image 1, vertical 9:16, talking-head podcast production.

AVOID: changed face, hairstyle, or outfit; added, removed, or moved props; different lighting; extra people; distorted hands; warped background; text; cartoon or CGI look.
```

10. Low Angle

```plain text
Using Image 1 as the master scene reference and Image 2 as the character identity reference, recreate the exact same moment from a completely different camera position.

CAMERA: Extreme low angle worm's-eye view. The camera is physically resting on the floor directly in front of the subject, about 1 meter from her feet, lens tilted steeply upward at roughly 60–70 degrees, looking up at her. Wide-angle 18–24mm lens.

REQUIRED VISUAL RESULT OF THIS CAMERA POSITION:
- Strong upward perspective with vertical lines converging toward the top of the frame; walls, shelves, posters, and the arcade cabinet lean inward.
- Foreshortening: her shoes and legs appear large and close in the foreground, her torso narrows upward, and her head appears smaller and higher in the frame.
- The underside of her chin and jawline is visible; the face is seen from below, not straight on.
- The ceiling occupies the upper portion of the frame, and ceiling edges plus wall tops are clearly visible.
- The underside of the stool and the base of the microphone stand are visible near the bottom of the frame; the microphone stand towers upward.
- The rug/floor texture appears only at the very bottom edge, extremely close to the lens and slightly out of focus.
- Subject tilts her eyes slightly downward to look into the lens below her.

KEEP IDENTICAL TO IMAGE 1:
- Environment: same room, layout, furniture, props, wall decor, colors, and object positions. Treat the room as a real 3D space; only the camera moves, with physically correct perspective and parallax from floor level.
- Lighting: same light sources, direction, color temperature, intensity, contrast, and mood. Because the camera looks up, light falls on her face from above and in front as in Image 1, with natural shadows under the chin and nose consistent with that lighting.
- Character: exact same person as Image 2. Same face structure, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, skin texture, makeup, hairstyle, hair color, hair length, and hair volume. Facial identity is the highest priority, still recognizable from below.
- Outfit: same clothing, accessories, fabric textures, patterns, and fit.
- Pose: same seated posture on the stool, same hand placement, same expression, as if captured at the same instant by a second camera placed on the floor.
- Microphone/equipment: same physical position relative to the subject.

STYLE: Photorealistic, cinema camera look, natural skin texture, realistic wide-angle lens characteristics, color grading matching Image 1, vertical 9:16, talking-head podcast production.

AVOID: eye-level or chest-height camera, mild low angle, flat perspective, straight vertical lines, ceiling not visible, face seen straight on, changed face, hairstyle, or outfit; added, removed, or moved props; different lighting; extra people; distorted hands; warped facial features; text; cartoon or CGI look.
```
