## Motion Control — Senjata Affiliate Fashion

<mark class="brush-yellow">Motion Control</mark> adalah teknik paling efektif di ekosistem affiliate fashion karena alurnya sangat terstruktur:

1. **Cari Video Referensi**: Temukan video gerakan dinamis / OOTD di TikTok atau Reels.
2. **Ganti Karakter**: Terapkan identitas model AI menggunakan Character Sheet.
3. **Ganti Outfit**: Pasang pakaian produk yang ingin dipromosikan.
4. **Generate & Distribusi**: Hasilkan video motion, posting, dan tautkan ke keranjang kuning affiliate.

Simpel, cepat, dan hasilnya langsung siap pakai untuk konten penjualan.

## Jebakan "Motion Control Unlimited"

Banyak pembuat konten tergoda iklan "Motion Control Unlimited". Di sinilah kita perlu mengingat kembali fondasi [Mental Creator AI](/bab-1-fundamental/02-mental-creator-ai/):

> **Zaman sekarang, teknologi AI generasi video berkualitas tinggi membutuhkan komputasi GPU besar dan tidak ada yang benar-benar gratis.**

Layanan "unlimited" murah biasanya hanya trik mengakali kuota yang cepat mati (KOID). 

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Buang Jauh-Jauh Mindset "Gratisan":**
> Bukan berarti tidak boleh memakai kuota gratis — sangat bagus untuk fase eksplorasi dan riset. Namun untuk skala komersial, <mark class="brush-yellow">anggap tools AI sebagai investasi biaya produksi</mark>, bukan beban pengeluaran.

> - Video referensi *full-body* (seluruh tubuh) → foto karakter wajib *full-body*.

### Formula Prompt Motion Control (Anti-Morphing)

Gunakan prompt pengunci ini di kolom teks prompt video generator untuk hasil presisi tinggi:

- <mark class="brush-yellow">Identity Lock</mark> → Mengunci 100% wajah, pori-pori, dan undertone kulit dari foto model.
- <mark class="brush-blue">Camera Lock</mark> → Mengunci tripod dan focal length agar tidak goyang.
- <mark class="brush-purple">Motion Only</mark> → Hanya mengekstrak timing dan kinetika kerangka tubuh dari video.

```javascript
The uploaded character image is the ONLY identity source. The reference video is used ONLY for body motion, dance timing, rhythm, pose transitions, head movement, arm movement, hand movement, leg movement, weight shift and facial expression timing.

Negative:
identity drift, face swap, face morph, reference face leakage, different face, different skin, different hairstyle, different eye shape, different nose, different lips, different jawline, different body, plastic skin, over smooth skin, beautified face, CGI skin, anime skin, blurry face, flickering face, unstable identity, frame inconsistency, camera drift, camera shake, handheld, pan, tilt, zoom, dolly, orbit, truck, push in, pull out, tracking shot, reframing.

Use only a sharp, high-quality reference video with full body visible, stable camera, good lighting, minimal compression and minimal motion blur.

REFERENCE VIDEO
If body movement suggests camera movement, ignore it and keep the camera locked.
Only the character moves. The background remains perfectly stable.
No pan, tilt, zoom, dolly, truck, orbit, handheld movement, camera shake, push in, pull out, reframing, subject tracking, focal length change, framing change or perspective change.
Lock the camera from the first frame. Use a completely static tripod camera.

CAMERA LOCK
Identity consistency is more important than motion accuracy. If motion conflicts with identity, always preserve identity.
Never beautify, stylize, smooth skin, modify facial anatomy, age, ethnicity, hairstyle or body proportions.
Maintain realistic skin with natural pores, micro texture, peach fuzz, fine facial hair and natural imperfections.
Preserve exactly: face shape, bone structure, jawline, forehead, cheeks, chin, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, iris, nose, lips, ears, hairline, hairstyle, hair color, skin tone, skin undertone, skin texture, pores, freckles, moles, beauty marks, wrinkles, neck, head proportions and body proportions.
Keep the uploaded character 100% identical in every frame.
Treat the reference video as skeleton motion only. Never transfer face, identity, body shape, hairstyle, skin, makeup, clothing, age, gender, ethnicity or facial proportions from the reference.
```
